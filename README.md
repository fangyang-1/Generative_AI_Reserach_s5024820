#1.Storage and Accessibility: Explain why it is important to store and organise your files

efficiently. (2-3 sentences)

Efficient file storage and organisation make documents easy to find, access, and share when needed. It also reduces the risk of losing important work, duplicating files, or using outdated versions.

2.File Storage Methods: Specify which data types may require special access controls. (2-3

sentences)

Quantitative and qualitative analysis files may need special access controls because they can contain confidential participant data or sensitive results. Drafts, reports, literature reviews, and additional materials may also need restricted access if they include unpublished research, copyrighted sources, or identifying information.

3.Why is it important to have a good naming conven6on?

A good naming convention makes files easy to identify, search for, and sort correctly. It reduces confusion between versions, prevents accidental use of outdated files, and helps others understand the file contents quickly.
